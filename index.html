About Me

Research-led. Built, not just proposed.

I am a UX researcher and product designer with a multidisciplinary background in fashion and User Experience Engineering. My experience in fashion management developed my sensitivity to emerging trends, visual aesthetics, and consumer behaviour. These skills influence how I observe users, recognise behavioural patterns, and understand both user and client needs within digital product development.

In my UX practice, I use a range of research methods, including interviews, focus groups, eye tracking, card sorting, and usability testing, to understand user needs and identify the causes behind their pain points. I translate these insights into information architecture, user flows, product features, and interface designs, refining each solution through prototyping and iteration.

Combining analytical research with a strong visual perspective allows me to approach design from both functional and aesthetic viewpoints. I aim to create products grounded in real user behaviour, responsive to changing market needs, and communicated through clear, intuitive design.

My previous experience in buying and product development also taught me to work with data, suppliers, and cross-functional teams while balancing customer expectations with commercial goals, quality standards, and delivery constraints. This experience now helps me consider not only whether a design is useful and intuitive, but also whether it is feasible, relevant to the market, and capable of supporting wider business objectives.

Critique AI

◆ Nominated — UX Design Awards 2026

Role UX Researcher & Product Designer
Team Group project
Tools Figma, Claude code

CritiqueAI is a group project nominated for the UX Design Awards 2026. It explores how people can use generative AI without becoming overly dependent on its answers or losing control of their own thinking.

We designed a browser extension that works alongside existing AI chat platforms and adds critical thinking support directly into the user's usual workflow. When the extension identifies a claim that may require closer attention, it flags the relevant text and opens a side panel with four tools: Prompt Suggestion, Source & Evidence, Counter Argument, and Think-Board. These features help users check where information comes from, consider different perspectives, and organise evidence before forming their own conclusions.

The aim of CritiqueAI is to preserve the speed and convenience of generative AI while encouraging people to engage more critically with its responses, particularly during academic research and writing.

VLE Redesign

● Ongoing — completing September 2026

Role UX Researcher & Product Designer
Team Individual project in collaboration with the university's IT Department
Tools Figma, Claude Code

The VLE Redesign is an ongoing individual UX project developed in collaboration with the university's IT Department and scheduled for completion in September 2026. It explores how students in the Computing Department use the Virtual Learning Environment and identifies opportunities to improve their overall experience.

The project use Double Diamond process to move from problem discovery to design development and validation. My research includes two focus groups, card sorting, co-design activities, usability testing, and a literature review. These methods have helped me understand students' navigation behaviour, study habits, file-management practices, and expectations of how course information should be organised.

The redesign focuses on improving the platform's navigation, macro and micro information hierarchy, course-page structure, content grouping, and file organisation. The aim is to create a clearer and more consistent experience that helps students find what they need more efficiently and reduces unnecessary effort during their studies.

School Mascot Project

Role Mascot Designer & Project Coordinator
Team Collaboration with the Student Union and manufacturer
Project type Charity project

The School Mascot Project was a charity initiative developed in collaboration with the Student Union to raise funds for a local disability support centre. Together, we created a mascot that could represent the school community, choosing a rabbit as the final character and turning the concept into a series of 200 plush toys for a fundraising sale.

I worked across the project from early concept development and mascot design to manufacturer communication, material selection, quality control, and promotion. The process gave me practical experience of how a physical product moves from an initial idea into production, as well as how design decisions need to consider cost, materials, manufacturing limitations, and audience expectations.

The project showed me how design, production, and community engagement can work together to support a shared cause.

Photography Project: Blindness and Illusion

Role Photographer, Art Director & Photo Editor
Team Individual project
Tools Adobe Photoshop

Blindness and Illusion is a photography project exploring how perception can be shaped, restricted, or misled. Through staged images, unusual perspectives, lighting, and composition, I experimented with ways of making familiar subjects feel uncertain or difficult to interpret.

I tried to use a more considered approach to photography in this project. Rather than treating each photograph as an individual image, I learned how to plan a visual concept, control technical decisions, and arrange a sequence of photographs to communicate a story.

This project strengthened my understanding of visual storytelling and taught me to make photographic decisions with greater intention, from the initial concept and shot planning to the final image sequence.

Alpha Brand Logo Redesign

Team Individual project
Project type Concept proposal
Tools Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop

The Alpha Brand Logo Redesign is a concept proposal for a traditional Taiwanese salon-grade haircare brand. The project explores how the brand could communicate a more professional and premium identity while remaining distinct from everyday consumer haircare products.

The logo concept was inspired by the Queen's Head rock formation in Yehliu, Taiwan. I combined its curved silhouette with the ideas of lightness, movement, and flowing hair to create a mark that suggests volume and smoothness. The visual direction was kept clean and minimal to suit Alpha's B2B audience and make the logo easy to recognise across packaging and professional salon materials.

Through this proposal, I explored how cultural references, brand positioning, and product characteristics can be translated into a clear visual identity.

Fashion E-commerce UI/UX Design

Team Individual project
Project type Creative exploration
Tools Figma

This interface prototype was one of my first creative explorations after transitioning from fashion into UX design. I wanted to understand how ideas from fashion presentation could be translated into a digital shopping experience, while still keeping the interface familiar and easy to use.

Inspired by the movement of a runway show, I designed a curved product carousel that allows users to browse items through a more fluid and visually expressive interaction. I also introduced short review highlights so users could quickly scan practical information about sizing, quality, and fit without reading through long comment sections.

Although the project focused on experimentation, I retained familiar scrolling and navigation patterns to avoid making the experience unnecessarily complicated. It helped me begin connecting my understanding of fashion, consumer behaviour with interaction design and usability.

Smart Home App UI/UX Design

Team Individual project
Project type Vibe-coding
Tools Figma, Windsurf

This smart home app was a vibe-coding prototype focused on turning a product concept into a working prototype within a short timeframe. I began by writing a complete product requirements document that defined the app's purpose, target users, core features, user flows, and interface requirements.

Using the PRD and my initial wireframe ideas, I worked with AI-assisted coding tools to build and refine the interface. The prototype allows users to manage connected home devices, monitor their status, and access key controls from one place.

The project helped me explore how designers can use structured documentation and AI-assisted development to communicate ideas more quickly.
